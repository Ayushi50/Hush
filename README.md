# Hush

A quiet night pond for anxious minds — a calming browser game with no timers, no scores to beat and no way to lose.

Meet **Lumi**, a little glowing pond spirit who breathes with you, gathers fireflies, and carries your worries up to the stars.

## Modes

- **Breathe** — Lumi swells and softens with your breath. Three patterns: Calm (4·2·6), Box (4·4·4·4), Sleep (4·7·8).
- **Drift** — Touch the water to make ripples and notes. Draw fireflies close and they bloom into lotuses.
- **Let go** — Type a worry; it becomes a paper lantern that floats away and turns into a distant star.

## Games

- **Koi Pond** — Tap the water to drop food and watch the koi glide over.
- **Echo** — Lumi hums a short tune on five glowing pads; hum it back. Mistakes just mean "listen once more".
- **Bubbles** — Pop drifting glow orbs; golden ones release a kind word.
- **Stargazer** — Join stars into your own constellations.

## Sound

All audio is generated live with the Web Audio API — a warm ambient pad, soft water and pentatonic chimes, so every note sounds pleasant together. Best with headphones.

## Run it

It's a single self-contained file. Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or enable **GitHub Pages** (Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`) to get a shareable link.
